Support for Rakugaki Grand Prix.

Contact us

Include your app version, device, OS, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers, or identity documents. Remove personal information from any screenshots.

info@arkartworks.jp

Remove Ads purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase. Check your store's confirmation screen for the price. It removes standard interstitial ads. Rewarded ads and ads required to race against imported or shared designs are excluded.

Restore purchases

Use the same store account used for your purchase and choose purchase restoration in the game settings. Purchases do not transfer between different operating systems or stores.

Optional AI model

The game works without downloading the optional AI model. The download is approximately 2.6 GB. Check available storage and use a stable Wi-Fi connection. If it fails, contact us with the message shown in the game.

Sharing designs

Share the original PNG exported by the game. Recompression, cropping, or editing by social networks may prevent restoration. The game has no online multiplayer or public post feed.

Move your designs and progress

In versions with backup support, use Settings to export a backup containing vehicle images, garage, GP, race history, and unfinished drawings. Import it on another device to replace that device's current progress; back up its existing data first. Purchases, age selection, ad and analytics consent, and ad rewards do not transfer. Restore purchases through the destination store. The backup contains your drawings and vehicle names, so check where you save or share it.
